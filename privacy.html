Privacy Policy

Phuong Studio Publisher · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Phuong Studio Publisher ("the app", "we") is an internal tool operated by Huỳnh Thanh Phương to schedule and upload videos to YouTube channels that we own or manage. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, stored and deleted, and how you can revoke access.

1. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Data we access

When a channel owner connects a YouTube channel through Google sign-in, the app can access:

  • Basic channel information: channel ID and channel name, to show which channel is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos and to set or update their title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status and scheduled publish time.
  • The status of videos uploaded by the app (for example processing state and publish status), to confirm they were published.

The app does not access comments, subscribers, analytics, payments, contacts, email or any other Google account data.

3. How we use the data

The data is used only to upload and schedule our own videos on the connected channel and to confirm that they were published. It is not used for advertising, profiling, selling, or any purpose unrelated to that function.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on the computer or server we operate that runs the app, and are used only by the app. They are kept until access is revoked or the channel is disconnected.
  • Upload records (video ID, title and scheduled time) are kept for no more than 30 days, then deleted.
  • The app stores data on the device it runs on (local files and, where a browser is used to sign in, cookies needed for sign-in). It uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through YouTube API Services with any third party. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, as needed to perform the uploads.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • When access is revoked or a channel is disconnected, we delete the stored tokens and any upload records for that channel within 7 days.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to the contact email below.

7. Security

Tokens are stored only on systems we control, are never published, and are never sent to anyone other than Google.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
