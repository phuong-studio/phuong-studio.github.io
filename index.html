Phuong Studio Publisher

An internal tool that lets Huỳnh Thanh Phương schedule and upload videos to the YouTube channels we own.

What the app does

  • Signs in to our own YouTube channels with Google OAuth (each channel owner grants access once).
  • Uploads finished videos from our own computer to the selected channel.
  • Sets the video title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the status of videos it uploaded, so we can confirm they were published on time.

Who uses it

The app is used only by Huỳnh Thanh Phương and our team, for channels we own or manage. It is not offered, sold or distributed to the public.
